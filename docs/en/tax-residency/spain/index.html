Why plan it

Why your arrival in Spain
needs to be designed.

  • ✓The special Beckham law regime lets you be taxed as a non-resident for 6 years, but it must be requested in time and has strict requirements.
  • ✓The time of year you become resident changes your entire tax bill, timing matters as much as structure.
  • ✓Your existing structures (LLC, companies, crypto, investments) should be reviewed before the move, not after.
Requirements

What we review
with you.

  • ✓Your starting point, current country, income and existing structures
  • ✓Whether you qualify for the Beckham law or other special regimes
  • ✓The optimal arrival timing and its impact on the current tax year
  • ✓Reporting obligations you'll have as a resident (Form 720 and similar)
