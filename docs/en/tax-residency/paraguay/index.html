Why Paraguay

Why so many entrepreneurs
choose Paraguay.

  • ✓Territorial tax system, income generated outside Paraguay is not taxed in Paraguay.
  • ✓Local income tax of 10%, one of the lowest on the continent.
  • ✓Permanent residency achievable quickly, without large investment requirements.
  • ✓No obligation to spend long periods in the country to keep your legal residency.
Requirements

What you need
to get started.
