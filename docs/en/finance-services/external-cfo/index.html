What it is

What an external CFO
actually does.

An external CFO is a finance director who works with your company on a recurring but part-time basis. They analyse your numbers, build your metrics, prepare the reporting and sit down with you every month to decide with data.

It's how growing companies access senior financial expertise while paying only for the time they actually need.
