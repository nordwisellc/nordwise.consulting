What it is

What an
FP&A setup is.

FP&A stands for financial planning and analysis. The setup means building, once and properly, your financial model, annual budget, projections and scenarios, and leaving you a system you can update every month without depending on anyone.

And if you need to present the business, a business plan with a solid narrative and coherent numbers makes all the difference.

What's included

What the setup
includes.

  • ✓A financial model tailored to your business, revenue, costs, margins and cash
  • ✓Annual budget and projections with scenarios (base, optimistic, prudent)
  • ✓A presentable business plan for banks, partners or investors if you need it
  • ✓Training so your team can update the model without external help
